Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Wiesticized Solitaire is designed as a local-only game. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, gameplay data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent to Wiesticized, Apple, Google, or any other party.

What data the app handles

The app stores the following locally on your device, in a file inside the app's private storage area:

  • Profile names you choose
  • Game statistics (games played, won, lost; cumulative counts; averages; distribution of end-of-game cards built; date of last win; history of recent games, capped at the 100 most recent)
  • In-progress game state so a game can be resumed if the app is closed and reopened

This data stays on your device. It is never transmitted off the device by the app under any circumstances. When you uninstall the app, this data is removed by your operating system along with the app.

Export and Import

The app lets you export your profiles and statistics to a JSON file. When you tap Export, the operating system's share sheet (iOS) or "Save As" dialog (Windows) opens, and you choose where the file goes — the app never sends the file anywhere on its own. Likewise, Import opens a file picker and only reads the file you select.

Network activity

The app does not make requests to any Wiesticized server (there is no such server) and does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking service.

One exception: the app uses the open-source google_fonts package to display its typography (Cormorant Garamond and Spectral). On first launch, this package downloads the font files from Google Fonts' public CDN (fonts.google.com) and caches them on your device. Subsequent launches do not re-fetch them. These requests are initiated by your device's standard web stack and include ordinary HTTP metadata such as your device's IP address and user-agent string. Google's own privacy policy for Google Fonts describes how it handles such requests; in summary, Google does not log the IP addresses of Google Fonts requests for analytics purposes.

Permissions requested

iOS asks the app to declare a few permissions in its manifest even for APIs the app does not use directly, because they are referenced by code in the file-picker library bundled with the app. The app declares these as "not used":

  • Photo Library — the app does not access your photo library
  • Camera — the app does not use the camera
  • Location — the app does not use your location

No permission prompts should appear during normal play.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data of any kind — from children or anyone else. It is suitable for all ages. The developer has no way to identify, contact, or communicate with any user through the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future update, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated "last updated" date. Material changes affecting what data the app handles will also be noted in the app's release notes.
