About the game

Wiesticized Solitaire is an original single-player patience card game played with two standard 52-card decks — 104 cards in total. The object is to move all 104 cards onto eight building piles, four built down from King to Ace and four built up from Ace to King, one of each direction per suit.

The deal is intricate — a 14-position rotation with four distinct stock-feeding mechanisms. Reaching the top of the stock, dealing a card onto its matching rank pile, and placing an Ace all divert extra cards into the stock before play begins. The result: every deal plays differently, and the stock pile becomes the engine that periodically unlocks full access to a tableau pile during the game.

Where to play

Wiesticized Solitaire is in private beta on iOS via TestFlight and available as a Windows desktop app. A public iOS App Store release is in preparation.

Design

One game, two platforms, one codebase. Written in Flutter with hand-built dealing and rules engines, local-only profiles and statistics, and no telemetry.
